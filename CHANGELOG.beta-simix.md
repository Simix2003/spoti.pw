### Known limitations / not changed

* **Spotify DJ page:** header and ⋯ sheet not restyled; no class for the DJ playlist page is proved in this checkout (no `trees/` dump, no binary), so nothing was hooked. Home's DJ card is only `_TtC30Discovery_MediumDensityCardKit9DJMDCView` (`Redesigned/Home/HomeCards.x`). Treating every sheet as the player menu would steal Share and other sheets those pages push.
* **Recently Added sort on mixed playlists:** not feasible safely; the redesigned ⋯ sheet already fires Spotify's Sort control. Mix stores transitions between adjacent tracks; Spotify's own reorder for that state is Smart Reorder, not Recently added. See `docs/mixed-playlist-sort.md`.
* **CarPlay:** research only; a sideloaded tweak cannot grow a CarPlay app (`com.apple.developer.carplay-audio` is restricted, `scripts/install.sh` re-signs with the user's profile). When Spotify's own CarPlay session is up, the current line is already in `MPMediaItemPropertyArtist`; CarPlay's audio rules say not to show lyrics on the car screen. See `docs/carplay-notes.md`.
* **Downloaded songs:** no per-song downloaded badge in the redesign; only the entity-level download button is restyled (`SGRDownload.m`). Playlist and album rows in `PlaylistRows.x` and `AlbumRows.x` are not given a download glyph.
